The manufacturer

Ningbo Fujin Garden & Irrigation Equipment Co., Ltd. was founded in 2008 in Yuyao, Zhejiang province. Its own R&D team covers electronics, software, mechanical engineering and product design.

Fujin products are used in home gardens, landscaping, greenhouses, farms and municipal green spaces.
